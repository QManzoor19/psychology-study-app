---
<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. Dendrites receive · cell body sums · axon transmits · terminal buttons release.
2. ~86 billion neurons; ~100 trillion connections (1,000–10,000 each).
3. From about 1 m/s to as much as 120 m/s — roughly a hundredfold.
4. No. It attacks the myelin sheath; the neurons are initially intact. Symptoms come from lost insulation.
5. Trick question. Older textbooks say 10:1. Direct counting puts it close to 1:1.
